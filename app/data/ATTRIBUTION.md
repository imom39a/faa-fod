# Image attribution (PoC test data)

Every Angle frame under `scenes/` is cut from a real, openly licensed photograph on Wikimedia Commons. The frames are modified by this project: cropped to 16:9 and resized, some mirrored left-to-right (ENG-L stand-ins), some with debris or a GSE vehicle composited in, some with a synthetic glare, blur or low-light degradation. Reference frames (`ref/`) and after frames (`after/`) are the same crops with simulated camera and lighting drift. Frames derived from CC BY-SA photographs are shared under the same licence as their source. A licence covers copyright only: identifiable people and airline liveries carry rights it does not cover, and nothing here implies endorsement by any airline, airport or photographer.

Built by `app/tools/build_scenes.py` from `app/tools/manifest/` (`sources.lock.json` holds the download URL and sha256 of every file). Per-scene detail (source crop box in source pixels, edits, objects) is in each scene's `build.json`. Scenes in the scoring set (`testset.json`): 30; other scenes are demo-only.
