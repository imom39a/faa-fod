## Question

For Hartsfield–Jackson Atlanta (ATL) Concourse T as the PoC's example deployment with Delta as the operating airline, find from public primary sources:
- the gate numbering and layout of Concourse T
- which gates Delta uses and the typical aircraft types (narrowbody: A320 family / 737 / A220 / 757)
- who controls the ramp at ATL (Delta ramp control vs the airport) and the airport's FOD program / Airport Certification Manual responsibilities as far as public
- the airline/airport responsibility split for gate areas, if public
- any public photos or diagrams of the Concourse T stands

Recommend **5 example gates** that give variety (e.g. end-of-concourse vs mid-concourse, different neighbours, a stand next to a taxi lane). Also list openly licensed photos of ATL Concourse T / Delta narrowbodies at gates usable in the test set or dashboard (licence + URL).

## Resolution

Findings: [research/atl-concourse-t-site.md](../research/atl-concourse-t-site.md).
- **Layout:** Concourse T is a north–south pier on the east face of the Domestic Terminal. All gates face east onto Ramp 1, the taxilane shared with Concourse A. T1 is the south end, T21 the north end. T17–T21 (2022 extension) are ADG III gates, mostly United. No public source lists all 21 gates.
- **Delta's gates:** T1–T3 confirmed (Delta 2017 release). Delta holding the whole south half is likely but unverified; the lease's gate exhibit isn't public.
- **Aircraft:** A220, A319/A320/A321, 717, 737-800/-900ER, 757.
- **Ramp control:** each ramp has its own frequency (Ramp 1: 131.45). No FAA or City document names the operator. Delta running Ramp 1 is probable, not verified. TBI staffs Ramps 3 and 4.
- **Who removes FOD at ATL:** the airline keeps its own stand clear (lease §12.01.D). The airlines jointly maintain the taxilane between stands through their contractor AATC (§12.01.B). Airport Operations removes FOD on the movement area, beyond the zipper marking.
- **Ramp rules:** no public ATL document mentions an equipment restraint line or safety envelope; those come from airline practice. Published rules: no parking under jet bridges, two wing walkers per push or arrival, violation classes for staging and unsecured FOD-producing items.
- **Recommended 5 gates:** T1, T2, T4, T5, T8 (reasons in the findings). The choice is confirmed in 0016.
- **Photos:** no openly licensed photo shows a numbered T gate at apron level. One good overview exists (CC BY-SA 4.0). The FAA airport diagram is public domain.
- Four questions for Delta's ATL station are listed at the end of the findings.
