## Question

Establish, from primary sources, the required and recommended FOD workflow for an airline at its gates, from pre-arrival to pushback. Cover:
- the steps and when each happens
- roles and authority: ramp agent, wing walker, pushback lead / headset operator, ramp control, Airport Operations
- hand-offs and escalation (airline vs airport boundary)
- GSE staging and equipment restraint line rules
- engine ingestion zone guidance
- what must be recorded

Separate **binding** requirements (14 CFR Part 139; FAA ACs as applied; any FAA rules on air carriers, e.g. 14 CFR 121 ground ops, and OSHA where relevant) from **industry standard** (IATA IGOM / AHM / ISAGO, NATA, airline ground-ops manuals if public) and **common practice**. Verify or correct the user-supplied turnaround practice recorded in the 0005 and 0006 amendments (pre-arrival walk, GSE staging check, cargo/baggage checks, final 360° walk, engine clear zone, headset clearance, escalation outside the gate). Output a workflow table with citations that the requirements baseline (0016) can adopt directly.

## Resolution

Findings: [research/gate-turnaround-fod-workflow.md](../research/gate-turnaround-fod-workflow.md), including a 15-step workflow table ready for 0016.
- **No federal rule prescribes a gate FOD workflow.** AC 00-34B says so outright. Part 121 only requires the ops manual to cover ground duties (§121.133/.135). Part 5 SMS keeps safety-assurance records for 5 years. OSHA covers ramp workers (29 CFR 1910.22, general). The FAA has no marking standard for gate stands (AC 150/5340-1M, AC 150/5300-13B §5.12.2 defer to industry).
- **Advisory FAA people-safety guidance:** AC 00-34B (engine ingestion zones), AC 00-65A (wing walkers, clear loose equipment before movement, walk-around before engine start), SAFO 23006. Boeing gives the 737NG intake hazard zone as 10 ft at idle and 14 ft at breakaway thrust.
- **IATA IGOM 4.1.1** (from IATA's free gap-analysis excerpts): FOD check of the entire stand just before arrival, and GSE, chocks and cones outside the equipment restraint area. The departure section (4.6) is paywalled and not verified.
- **ATL is binding on tenants:** its violations poster includes two wing walkers for pushback and arrival, staging, and securing FOD. Airport Operations removes FOD in the movement area.
- **Checking the user-supplied practice:**
  - Confirmed: pre-arrival walk, GSE staging (FOD-R9), cargo/baggage checks, engine clear zone, and dropping the post-block-in Inspection.
  - The 360° walk is confirmed, with a caveat.
  - **Corrections:** (1) pushback and engine start are cleared by the flight crew; the Ramp Lead confirms the area is clear and clears HOLDs. (2) FOD-R10 is too strong. Apron taxilanes are non-movement areas split by lease or agreement, so outside-envelope FOD goes to **ramp control and/or Airport Operations** per local agreement.
- **Left for 0016:** whether to keep a per-Turn FOD record (no source requires one), and the Turn definition, which currently starts at block-in and conflicts with pre-arrival.
