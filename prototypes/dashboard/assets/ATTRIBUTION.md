# Image attribution (dashboard prototype assets)

Every Angle frame in `img/` is a crop of a real, openly licensed photograph from Wikimedia Commons, resized to 1600×900. Some frames also have real photographed debris pasted in (FOD-A, see below) or a synthetic glare overlay; the table says which. The crops, debris compositing and glare are modifications by this project. Frames derived from CC BY-SA photos are shared under the same licence (CC BY-SA 3.0 / 4.0, as listed). Built by `../tools/build_assets.py` (crops in `SETS`, debris in `../tools/composites.json`). Ground-truth boxes are in `scenes.json` (and `scenes.js` for the page).
